Is my chat uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is processed inside your browser and never sent to a server.

How do I export a chat?

In WhatsApp open the chat, tap the menu or the contact name, choose Export chat, then Include media, and save the .zip.

What does the Free plan include?

Two conversions in total, text only: photos, voice notes and videos are not included. Paid plans include them.

How do I get my chat file?

In WhatsApp open the chat, tap the menu or contact name, choose Export chat, then Include media. Save the .zip file and upload it here.

Is my chat uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is processed inside your browser and never sent to a server.

The page freezes with a big zip. What can I do?

Large zips use a lot of memory. Try a desktop browser, or export fewer media files.

Voice notes do not play on my iPhone.

Voice notes use the .opus format, which some Safari versions cannot play. Open the page in Chrome or Firefox.

How do I pay and activate a plan?

Open Billing, choose a plan, pay with JazzCash or Easypaisa and send the transaction ID on WhatsApp. Your plan activates on your account after the payment is verified.

I paid but my plan is not active.

Payments are checked by hand. Send your transaction ID and name on WhatsApp support and we will sort it out.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Your chats stay with you

Chat Printer converts your exported WhatsApp chat inside your own browser. The chat file, the messages and the media are not uploaded to our servers.

What we collect

  • When you sign in: your email address (and the basic profile details Google shares, if you use Google sign-in) and a user ID.
  • Your plan, when it ends, and how many conversions you have used. We do not store your chat or your files.
  • When you submit a payment: the name you type, the payment method, the plan and the Transaction ID.

Who handles the data

Accounts, plans and payment records are stored with Appwrite Cloud. The website is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs. If you choose Google sign-in, Google handles that step. We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising or tracking scripts.

Browser storage

Your browser keeps a sign-in session so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and records at any time through the Contact page.
