Export a WhatsApp chat to PDF

Updated 18 November 2026 · 5 minute read

WhatsApp can export a chat as a text file, but it cannot make a nice PDF by itself. You export the chat first, then convert it. This guide shows both steps on Android and iPhone.

Step 1: Export the chat from WhatsApp

Android: open the chat, tap the three dots, choose More, then Export chat. Pick Include media if you want photos, voice notes and videos, or Without media for text only. Save the file to your phone or share it to yourself.

iPhone: open the chat, tap the contact or group name, scroll down and tap Export Chat. Choose Attach Media or Without Media, then save the file to the Files app.

With media, WhatsApp gives you a .zip file. Without media, you get a .txt file. Chat Printer reads both.

Step 2: Open Chat Printer and load the file

  1. Open Chat Printer and sign in.
  2. Choose the .zip or .txt file. Nothing is uploaded: the chat is read inside your browser.
  3. Pick which person is "me" so your messages appear on the right, like in WhatsApp.

Step 3: Choose what to include

  • Date range: set a start and an end date to print only those days.
  • Search: keep only messages that contain certain words.
  • System messages: hide notices such as "messages are end-to-end encrypted".
  • Theme: light or dark, with the familiar WhatsApp wallpaper.

Step 4: Download the PDF

Press Download PDF. In the print window choose Save as PDF as the printer. If the background looks plain, switch on Background graphics. A4 paper works well for most chats.

Common problems

  • The date format looks wrong: change the date format setting from DD/MM/YYYY to MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Names are missing in a group: only people who sent messages appear in the member line.
  • Deleted messages: WhatsApp removes their text from exports, so only a marker can be shown.
