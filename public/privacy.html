Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Your chats stay with you

Chat Printer converts your exported WhatsApp chat inside your own browser. The chat file, the messages and the media are not uploaded to our servers.

What we collect

  • When you create an account: your email address (and the basic profile details Google, Facebook, GitHub or TikTok share if you use them), a user ID and your sign-in sessions.
  • What you add to your profile: your name, an optional phone number (we do not verify it) and an optional photo.
  • Your plan, when it ends and how many conversions you have used. We do not store your chat or your files.
  • When you pay: the plan, amount, currency, payment method and Transaction ID, and the name you type for wallet payments.

Payments

Card payments are handled by our payment partner, which collects your card details on its own secure page; we never see your card number. For Pakistan wallet payments we receive the payment confirmation messages sent to our phone and keep them for up to 30 days to match your Transaction ID. These messages can contain the sender name or number.

Location and prices

We use your approximate country, worked out from your connection, to show prices in your currency. You can change the currency at any time. Our account provider records sign-in details such as device and approximate location so you can see and end your sessions in your account.

Who handles the data

Accounts, plans and payment records are stored with Appwrite Cloud. The website is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs. If you choose a social sign-in, that provider handles that step. We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising or tracking scripts.

Browser storage

Your browser keeps a sign-in session and your currency choice. Signing out removes the session.

Browser extension (Chat Printer Exporter)

The extension works only in your browser. When you press Export it reads the chat you have open on a supported site, builds the file on your computer and saves it. Your chats are never sent to us or to anyone else. To keep the exporter for signed-in users, the extension sends only your extension key to our server to verify your account and enforce your plan (one export per payment); it has no analytics and loads no remote code beyond the site's own public config file. It remembers your last export choices (format, look, names) and your key in your browser using the storage permission. It asks for access to the supported chat sites and their picture and video servers only so it can read the open chat and download its media when you ask. You can remove it at any time on your browser's extensions page.

Your choices

You can download your data, deactivate your account in your account page, or ask us to delete your records through the Contact page.
