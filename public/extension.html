Chat Printer Exporter: save chats from other apps

Browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave — free with your Chat Printer account

Chat Printer turns WhatsApp chats into PDF, PNG and HTML. For every other app, use this extension: it saves the chat you have open as a file, in the same WhatsApp-style look.

Works on

  • Messenger and Facebook (messenger.com, facebook.com)
  • Instagram direct messages
  • X (x.com, twitter.com) direct messages
  • Telegram Web (web.telegram.org)
  • LinkedIn messaging
  • Discord (discord.com)

Photos and videos are downloaded for Messenger, Facebook, Instagram and X. On Telegram Web, LinkedIn and Discord the text is exported and media depends on what the site allows, so some may be missing.

What you get

  • HTML: a chat page that looks like WhatsApp, light or dark, with the doodle wallpaper. Open it on any device.
  • TXT: a plain text file in WhatsApp style.
  • JSON: the messages as data.
  • With media: a ZIP with the chat and a media folder.

Install (about two minutes)

The extension is not in the browser stores yet, so you install it in Developer mode. This is safe for a file you trust: the checksum above lets you check that your download is the one we published.

  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it. You get a folder named chat-printer-exporter.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge edge://extensions).
  3. Switch on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the chat-printer-exporter folder.
  5. Open a chat on one of the sites above and refresh the page.
  6. Click the Chat Printer icon and sign in with your extension key (create one free on the website: Account → Browser extension). The exporter stays unlocked on your computer.
  7. Choose the format, media and look, then press Export this chat.
Tip: press Test detection first. Red outlines show which messages were found. If the wrong area is picked, use Pick chat area manually.

Your privacy

  • Everything runs inside your browser. Your chats are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, no ads, no remote code.
  • It remembers your last options (format, look, names) in your browser using the storage permission.
  • It asks for access to the sites above and their picture and video servers only so it can read the chat you open and download its media when you ask.

Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Good to know

  • Websites change. If a site redesigns its chat page, the export can stop working until we update the extension.
  • It saves the chat you have open, one chat at a time. Auto-scroll loads older messages first; very long chats take time.
  • Chrome-style browsers only. Firefox and Safari are not supported.
  • Your own chats only. Save chats you have the right to keep, and follow the rules of the platform. The extension scrolls the page like a person would; platforms may limit automated use, so use it at your own risk.
  • WhatsApp? Use the Chat Printer website instead.

Questions
